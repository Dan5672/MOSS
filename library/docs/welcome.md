- **Where things live:**
  - **Assets**: your devices, and what agents can do with each.
  - **Monitoring**: checks on your services; when one goes down, an agent responds.
  - **Incidents** and **Changes**: problems and fixes.
  - **Wiki**: what we've learned about your network.
  - **Chat**: talk to agents and people.
  - **Activity**: everything agents did, step by step.
- **Stay safe:** give agents read-only accounts where you can, and read a change before you approve it. The getting started guide has a section on this.
